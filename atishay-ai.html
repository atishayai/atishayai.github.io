Jarvis

Students burn out from disorganization, not from inability.

A one-stop command center for a student's whole college life: drag-and-drop your semester, track every major and minor requirement as a checklist, keep to-dos, deadlines, and notes in one place. The part I could not find anywhere else is the optimizer — it works out, live, which courses count toward several of your programs at once. Cornell's own tools do not do that.

It runs entirely in your browser. No account, no server, nothing of yours leaves your laptop. Built on the Fall 2026 roster: 4,464 courses and all 221 Cornell majors and minors. I am building it as its own first user — I transfer to Cornell this fall — and the goal is any student at any school.
