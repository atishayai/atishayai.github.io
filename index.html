about

i write essays and short fiction about the south asian diaspora, the architecture of personal identity, and the slow moral arithmetic of the machines that watch us.

my photographs are quieter than my sentences — kitchens at six o'clock, weeknight cities, strangers becoming briefly composed. most of what i publish begins as a frame i couldn't explain to myself; most of what i photograph begins as a sentence that wouldn't sit still.

i'm at work on my first collection.
