● What we never do

No diagnoses. No treatment claims. No risk scores sold to anyone. No insurer, employer, or school can ever be a customer for your data. And if your writing indicates crisis, analysis stops and the product connects you to human help — that path overrides everything, including your subscription.

Privacy & keys
A product this intimate wins or dies on trust — so the guarantees are structural, not policy.

🔑 Your archive, your key

Every archive is encrypted at rest with a per-user key. A breach of our database is not a breach of your corpus — the two are cryptographically separate.

🚫 Never trained on

Your writing is never used to train any model — ours or anyone's. It is analyzed on your behalf and readable by you alone. This is contractual, not a settings toggle.

🗑 Delete means delete

Deletion is cryptographic erasure: the key is destroyed, the ciphertext is unrecoverable, and derived analyses go with it. No soft-delete, no retention window.

👥 Other people in your writing

Your archive mentions people who didn't sign up. You can redact any person from analysis — their name, their details, every passage about them — and findings will never surface a third party's private information back to you as an insight.

🤝 A mirror, not a doctor

Longitude makes no diagnoses and no treatment claims. If your writing indicates crisis, the product stops analyzing and connects you to human help — that path overrides everything else.
